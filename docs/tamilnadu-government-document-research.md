Generated by `etl/content/build_reports.py` from `data/policy_documents/documents.json` (built by `etl/policy_documents/build_documents.py`).

## Method

1. Documents were discovered on **official Tamil Nadu listing pages** (not search engines): the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary 2026 list (stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in), the Rural Development and Panchayat Raj G.O. page (tnrd.tn.gov.in), the Municipal Administration G.O. page (tnurbantree.tn.gov.in), the Tamil Nadu State Judicial Academy circulars list (tnsja.tn.gov.in), and department sites hosting policy notes / G.O.s (forests.tn.gov.in, tnhb.tn.gov.in, dish.tn.gov.in).
2. Every PDF was **downloaded** and must be a real PDF. A record is `official_verified` only if its extracted text contains the expected identifier/subject words, or - for scanned PDFs without a text layer - if page 1 was rendered and its title, number and date were **checked by eye** against the record (recorded in `verification_method`).
3. Two candidates were replaced because the official link returned 404 on the research date (G.O. 5 of the RDPR list, which is why G.O. 58 is used instead; and a guessed Act file name, corrected from the listing) and one policy note (Labour) returned 403 and was dropped.
4. Where a check showed the listing description was wrong, the record follows the document: G.O. 105 (2020) is an **Adi Dravidar and Tribal Welfare** order (funeral assistance Rs.2,500 to Rs.5,000), not a Municipal Administration order; G.O. 64 (2025) is a **Social Welfare and Women Empowerment** order dated 18.06.2025.

## Limits and uncertainty

* **Circulars** are circulars of the High Court of Madras / Tamil Nadu State Judicial Academy for the district judiciary; they concern citizen matters (victim compensation, maternity leave, bonded labour, tobacco ban, legal aid) indirectly. Department-issued welfare circulars were not found on a machine-readable official list in this pass.
* **Acts**: each Act is published in an Extraordinary Gazette issue that contains several Acts, so those Acts share one PDF. Publication in the Gazette is recorded as `legal_status`; the **date of coming into force is not confirmed** and `effective_on` is empty. Act 11 carries the year 2024 in its short title but is numbered Act 11 of 2026 in the Gazette listing - recorded as published.
* `superseded_status` is `unknown` for every document; no document was checked against later amendments.
* The gazette list was read for the 2026 volume only; earlier foundational Acts are not included.
